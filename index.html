Barbudo Shove v3

Bet how many of your pieces will cross the board. Not one more, not one fewer.

You are blue and move up. The rival is red and moves down. The number on each piece is its rank: higher beats lower.

2

The rival can't bid 2, because the two bids can't add up to 4.

    How to play

    Each player has 4 pieces, ranked 1 to 4 (the number you see on each one), facing each other on a 4×4 board. Before the first move, each player bets how many of their own pieces will cross the board. The two bets can't add up to 4.

    On your turn you do one of these two things:

    Advance one piece one step. If the square ahead is empty, you move into it. If you are at the far edge, the piece walks off and has crossed: +1 for you. If the square ahead is occupied, you can only enter it if your rank is higher than the piece there. Then you shove that piece, and every piece touching it in the direction you choose, one square sideways. A piece shoved off the side is lost and counts for nobody. With an equal or lower rank, your piece is blocked.

    Sidestep (you get 3 in the whole game): move one of your pieces one square left or right, into an empty square or swapping places with another of your pieces. Use it to rearrange your ranks, or to wait a turn.

    Pieces never move backward, so the game always ends. If a player has no legal move they pass. If nobody can move, the pieces left on the board are stuck and count for nobody.

    Whoever ends closest to their bet wins. Going over is as bad as falling short, and a tie in distance is a draw.

    You are not forced to cross. Pieces you don't want to cross can be shoved off the side by your rival, who might do it if it helps them. And your rival can leave you no safe moves, so a piece you wanted to keep has to walk across.
